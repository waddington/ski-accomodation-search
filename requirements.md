# Requirements

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_


We need a catered chalet (ideally with a hot tub) for 8–10 people, 20–27 March 2027, at about £750 per person, in a resort that is part of a major linked ski area.

| Requirement | Detail | Must-have or preference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dates | Sat 20 – Sat 27 March 2027, 7 nights | Must-have |
| Group size | 8 people; 10-person chalets also acceptable at the same £750pp | Must-have (8); 10 is an option |
| Budget | About £750 per person for accommodation + catering (excludes flights/transfers): £6,000 for 8, £7,500 for 10 | Target; it is tight but achievable |
| Board | Catered | Must-have for chalets; relaxed if we broaden to other accommodation |
| Hot tub | Jacuzzi or hot tub at the chalet | Nice-to-have (was a must-have until 2026-10-05) |
| Ski area | A resort or village that is part of a major linked ski area | Must-have |
| Country | The Alps: France first, then Austria, Switzerland or Italy | Preference |
| Operators | Has booked with Icebreaker Chalets before | Starting point |

**What counts as a major ski area:** examples are Les Coches in Paradiski (where you've stayed before) and Tignes Les Brévières in Espace Killy (currently being looked at). Similar examples are satellite villages in the 3 Vallées, Portes du Soleil, Grand Massif, Ski Arlberg, SkiWelt, Monterosa, Milky Way and Matterhorn ski paradise. Satellite villages are usually where £750 per person is achievable.

**Still to confirm:** the bedroom split (doubles, twins or singles), the departure airport and the skiing level.
