# Broad search / aggregator sites

_Generated from [sites.csv](sites.csv) by build_sites_md.py · 42 sites_

Sites that list chalets from many operators or owners. Useful for every tier.

Best starting points: **Sno** (hot tub, budget and group-size filters), **allChalets** (catered and jacuzzi filters), **Ski Line**, **Iglu Ski**, **ChaletFinder**, **Heidi**, **Erna Low**. Discovery sites at the bottom are directories for finding more small operators, not booking engines.

Tiers: 1 catered chalet · 2 chalet hotel/club · 3 self-catered chalet · 4 apartment/hotel · discovery = directory. Sorted: in-budget first, luxury/high next, directories last.
