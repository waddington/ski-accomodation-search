# Search plan


We search in tiers. We only move down a tier if the one above produces nothing that fits the requirements.

1. **Tier 1: catered chalet with a hot tub.** Start with the specialist chalet companies, beginning with Icebreaker. Then use the broad search sites to fill gaps.
2. **Tier 2: chalet hotel or club hotel with a spa.** These are catered, but the hot tub or spa is shared. Mostly sold by the same chalet companies and tour operators.
3. **Tier 3: self-catered chalet with a hot tub.** The budget can then cover meals out or a visiting chef. Covered by the broad search sites plus the self-catered specialists.
4. **Tier 4: apartments or hotels with a pool or spa.** These are residences and hotels in the same linked ski areas.

The website lists in `sites/` are grouped to match: broad search sites cover every tier, chalet companies cover tiers 1 and 2, and the fallback list covers tiers 3 and 4.
