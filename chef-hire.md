# Chef hire for a self-catered chalet

_Checked 2026-10-05. Idea: rent a self-catered chalet (often cheaper, far more choice) and hire a chef to do what a catered chalet does._

| Service | Covers | What it is | Price found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Take a Chef — Tignes](https://www.takeachef.com/en-fr/private-chef/tignes) | Tignes, Val d'Isère, Courchevel, Méribel, Megève, Chamonix (and other cities) | Marketplace: chefs send menu proposals, you pick one | **€83 per person per meal for 7–12 guests** (€124 for 2–6), incl. ingredients, cooking and kitchen clean-up; drinks and tip extra. French page: [chef-prive/tignes](https://www.takeachef.com/fr-fr/chef-prive/tignes) |
| [The Alpine Chef Collective](https://www.alpinechefs.com/) | Val d'Isère, Tignes, **Les Brévières**, Sainte-Foy, La Rosière, Les Arcs | Collective of private chefs matched to chalets for week-long stays | Not published ("full site coming soon"); enquiry form |
| [Chalet Chef](https://www.chaletchef.com/) | Val d'Isère and Tignes (winter) | Freelance chef, 20+ years; breakfast, lunch or dinner, 7 days a week | Not published; quotes via info@chaletchef.com |
| [La Belle Assiette](https://labelleassiette.fr/) | France (city-focused) | Private-chef marketplace | Resort pages not found for Tignes/Morzine/Val Thorens — probably not useful in the mountains |

Also worth knowing:
- Some chalet listings in our data are **catered-or-self**: the owner offers optional catering (e.g. La Source in Les Coches/Montchavin, several allChalets listings). These are the easiest version of this idea.
- Freelance seasonaire chefs advertise on resort Facebook groups and the See-resort guide sites (e.g. See Tignes) — typically cheaper than marketplaces.

## Rough maths for 10 people, 7 nights

| Option | Per person |
| --- | --- |
| Take a Chef, 6 dinners at €83 | ~€500 (~£430) |
| + simple breakfasts bought yourselves | ~€60 (~£50) |
| Self-catered chalet with hot tub at €5,000–5,800/week (pilot: Brévières) | €500–580 (~£430–500) |
| **Total** | **~£910–980pp** — over £750 at marketplace rates |

So it beats £750pp only with a cheaper self-catered chalet (~€3,500 for 10) or a cheaper local freelance chef (~€40–60pp/dinner, unverified). Get quotes from the Alpine Chef Collective and Chalet Chef for a real comparison.
