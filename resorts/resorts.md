# Alpine ski resorts for a catered chalet, 20–27 March 2027

_Researched 5 October 2026. For a group of 8, catered chalet with hot tub, budget about £750 per person, Saturday 20 to Saturday 27 March 2027 (the week before Easter, which is 28 March)._

This file lists every village we found that sits on a major lift-linked ski area in the Alps, with stats for each area and a judgement on late-March snow, catered-chalet prices and UK operator presence. It does not look at specific chalets or availability. Every area listed is scheduled to be open on 20–27 March 2027, except Sixt (Grand Massif), whose own lifts close on 7 March. 20–27 March is late season, so the run home to low villages is the main risk.

## How to read this

- **Major linked area:** roughly 150 km or more of lift-linked pistes. A few near-misses (140–150 km) are kept and flagged as borderline when the user named them, or when they are a benchmark resort: Sölden, Espace San Bernardo, Mayrhofen, Montafon, Galibier-Thabor and Monterosa. Everything else is under **Excluded** at the end of each country.
- **Calibre** compares each village with the user's benchmark (high Tignes, Val Thorens, Alpe d'Huez, Les Deux Alpes, Morzine, Avoriaz, Sölden, Val d'Isère).
  - **A:** at or above the benchmark: big area, high or snow-sure, well known.
  - **B:** close: a big linked area but lower or smaller, or a satellite of an A area.
  - **C:** below the benchmark.
  - Tables are sorted A first, then by late-March snow, then by price.
- **Late-March snow:**
  - **high:** village at about 1,600 m or more, glacier, or a reliable run home.
  - **medium:** good skiing up top, but the run home to the village may be slushy or closed by the afternoon.
  - **low:** the village sits below about 1,000 m, or the run home is usually shut by late March, so you download by lift.
- **Chalet price** is for catered chalets, measured against £750 per person.
  - **budget:** £750 is realistic, even at mid-range.
  - **mid:** achievable with a simpler chalet or a late-season deal.
  - **premium:** unlikely at £750 unless the property is basic.
  - These are judgements, not quotes.
- **Satellite value:** "yes" means the village gives access to the big area at lower prices than the headline resort.
- **Pistes and runs:**
  - Linked km and runs come from skiresort.com (skiresort.info now redirects there) or from the official area site.
  - Run columns are **run counts** (green/blue/red/black) where the official count was found. Where only kilometres were published, they are shown as km (e.g. "130 km").
  - skiresort.com's "easy" category covers green and blue together, so French figures given in km show greens as "incl. in blue".
- **Altitudes, transfers and operators:**
  - Area-level figures were checked against sources.
  - Village altitudes and transfer times are approximate (general knowledge, cross-checked where possible).
  - UK operator lists were verified only for Ski Total, Skiworld, Inghams and some SNO listings. The Ski Total, Esprit and Crystal sites would not load during research.
- **Lift-pass prices:** adult 6-day prices, 2025/26 unless 2026/27 is stated. Blank cells mean we could not verify the figure.
- **resorts_raw.csv** has one row per village with every field, and a blank `status` column for you to fill in.

## France

France has the highest purpose-built resorts and the most UK catered-chalet operators, so it is the natural place to start. The best late-March value is usually a **high satellite** of a big area (Les Coches, Plan Peisey/Vallandry, Les Boisses, Les Menuires), or a lower satellite where you accept a lift down at the end of the day (Champagny, Brides-les-Bains, Orelle, Samoëns). Late-March 6-day lift passes are cheaper in Portes du Soleil (spring rate from 20 March 2027) and Évasion Mont-Blanc.

## Austria

Austria's big linked areas are mostly lower than France's. Many villages sit at 600–1,000 m, so in late March the skiing up top is usually fine but the run home is often slushy or closed. Run figures here are **km by colour (blue/red/black)** as published by skiresort.com, not run counts. Austrian catered chalets are thinner on the ground than in France; Inghams' Austrian catered chalets are concentrated in St Anton and Obergurgl, and most UK operators elsewhere sell hotels.

## Suggested shortlist to start with

These are mostly A/B villages. They balance three things: a realistic shot at £750 pp catered, snow that should hold in the last full week of March, and enough UK catered-chalet supply (ideally with hot tubs) to give real choice. They are roughly in priority order.

| # | Village | Area | Calibre | Why it is on the list |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tignes Les Brévières | Espace Killy | B | Cheapest door into a 300 km glacier area that is open until May. The north-facing run home has snowmaking. Ski Total has a chalet there. Already being considered. |
| 2 | Tignes Les Boisses | Espace Killy | B | 1,850 m, so snow is more secure than Les Brévières at a similar price level. Few beds, so check early. |
| 3 | Les Menuires (incl. Reberty, Les Bruyères) | 3 Vallées | A | 1,850–2,000 m and ski-in/out, with Val Thorens next door. The cheapest high village in the 600 km 3 Vallées. |
| 4 | Les Coches | Paradiski | B | Proven with the group. Holds snow better than Montchavin, and good-value catered chalets (Crystal and independents). |
| 5 | Plan Peisey / Vallandry | Paradiski | B | 1,600 m, ski-in/out, next to the Vanoise Express. Satellite prices with access to all 425 km. |
| 6 | Plagne 1800 / Plagne Centre | Paradiski | A | 1,800–1,970 m with a large chalet stock. Late-season chalet deals often come in near £750. |
| 7 | Arc 1800 | Paradiski | A | The main UK chalet hub in Les Arcs (Skiworld and others). High enough for late March. |
| 8 | Méribel-Mottaret | 3 Vallées | A | 1,750 m and central in the 3 Vallées. Ski Total is present. Mid-priced for an A village. |
| 9 | Val Thorens | 3 Vallées | A | The most snow-sure village on the list. It is mid–premium, so look at older or smaller chalets and late-season offers. |
| 10 | Alpe d'Huez, with Oz-en-Oisans / Vaujany as the satellite fallback | Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine | A/B | A benchmark resort with Skiworld, Inghams and Ski Total. Vaujany has a strong independent chalet scene and a cable car up to 2,800 m. |
| 11 | Les 2 Alpes | Les 2 Alpes | A | Glacier to 3,600 m and open until 2 May. Skiworld, Inghams and Ski Total are all present. Mid-priced. |
| 12 | Morzine, or Avoriaz for snow | Portes du Soleil | A | The largest supply of £750 pp catered chalets with hot tubs in the Alps, plus the cheapest lift pass that week (€299 from 20 Mar 2027). The catch is that Morzine is low, so you will ride the lifts down. |
| 13 | Sölden | Ötztal | A | A benchmark resort with two glaciers and a season that runs to May. Inghams is there, and chalets are mid-priced for Austria. |
| 14 | Breuil-Cervinia | Matterhorn Ski Paradise | A | 2,050 m, with the glacier link to Zermatt at Italian prices. Mostly hotels, so check the catered supply. |
| 15 | Selva / Wolkenstein | Sella Ronda | A | Inghams' Chalet Soldanella and Skiworld are there. Snowmaking is excellent, but Val Gardena closes on 4 April 2027, so this is the season's last fortnight. |

**Why some well-known names are left out:**
- **SkiWelt, KitzSki, Montafon, Évasion Mont-Blanc, Espace Diamant:** too low for late March.
- **Courchevel 1850, Verbier, Zermatt, Lech/Zürs, St Moritz:** too expensive.
- **Sybelles, Forêt Blanche, Espace Lumière:** almost no UK catered chalets.
